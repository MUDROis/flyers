Сегодня мы выучили Past continuous — длительное действие в прошлом: I was feeling happy, The sun was shining brightly, The children were walking to school. Формула: was/were + V-ing. А также союз while: While Harry was walking through the woods, he hurt his foot.

5

Reading. True or False?

Прочитай текст и отметь, верны ли утверждения: напиши True или False. Если False — исправь предложение (доп. звёздочка!). Незнакомые слова ищи в Wordlist учебника.

A strange night
Last summer we were camping near a big lake. In the evening we were sitting near the fire and were listening to stories. Suddenly we heard a strange noise in the woods. «What was it?» whispered Holly. While we were looking for the torch, we saw two big eyes in the dark. It was only a small furry animal — a badger! We laughed and went to sleep.
  • Last summer the family was camping near a big lake.
  • In the evening they were listening to stories near the fire.
  • They saw two big eyes in the dark.
  • It was a big bear.
6

Writing. Мини-сочинение.

Напиши «Вчера в 7 вечера…» — 5 предложений: что ты и твоя семья делали вчера вечером (was/were + V-ing).

Plan (use these prompts):
  • Yesterday at seven o'clock I was…
  • My mum was…
  • My dad was…
  • We were… while…
7

Speaking task. Расскажи о себе (запиши аудио).

Это устное задание. Подготовь ответ (30–60 секунд), запиши на телефон и отправь преподавателю до следующего занятия.

Plan for your speaking (use these prompts):
  • What were you doing at 8 o'clock yesterday evening?
  • What was your family doing?
  • Tell a mini-story: While I was…, something happened.
Example: “At eight o'clock yesterday evening I was playing chess with my brother. My mum was reading, and my dad was working on the computer. While I was going to bed, I heard a funny noise — it was my cat running in the hall!”
