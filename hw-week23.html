Conversation + Grammar: Present perfect с just, already — 7 упражнений, 20–25 минут

Учебник
Get Ready for Flyers (2nd ed.)
Страницы
SB стр. 66
Уровень
A1 → A2
Время
20–25 минут

Сегодня мы начали изучать Present perfect — время для «только что» и «уже» сделанных действий: I've just had my lunch, You've already done your homework. Формула: have/has + третья форма глагола (V3).

КатегорияСлова и выражения
Present perfect: justI've just had my lunch. / The match has just finished.
Present perfect: alreadyI've already had my lunch. / You've already done your homework.
V3 глаголыdo–done, eat–eaten, finish–finished, have–had, be–been, see–seen

4

Grammar practice. Практика грамматики.

Ответь, используя just и слова в скобках (have/has + V3).

  • Would you like some tea? (just / have a cup of tea)
  • Let's watch the film! (already / see it)
  • Is Kate hungry? (just / have a snack)
  • Do your homework! (already / do it)

After the match
The football match has just finished. Ben is very happy: his team has won 3:2! «Have you called your dad?» asks Harry. «Yes, I've already told him the news», says Ben. «And I've just drunk a big bottle of water — I'm very thirsty after the game. Let's have a snack in the café!»
  • The match has just finished.
  • Ben's team has won 3:2.
  • Ben has already called his dad.
  • Ben is hungry, not thirsty.

7

Speaking task. Расскажи о себе (запиши аудио).

Это устное задание. Подготовь ответ (30–60 секунд), запиши на телефон и отправь преподавателю до следующего занятия.

Plan for your speaking (use these prompts):
  • What have you already done today?
  • What have you just done?
  • What are you going to do after the lesson?
Example: “Today I have already had breakfast and done my homework. I have just finished reading an interesting book. After the lesson I am going to play football with my friends in the yard.”
