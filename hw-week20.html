The school trip plan
Tomorrow class 5 is going to go to the old castle. «What are we going to see there?» asks Harry. «The gold crown and the king's rooms», says the teacher. «Take your jackets — it might be cold in the castle. And we might have a picnic in the garden if the weather is fine. Wear comfortable shoes — we are going to climb a lot of steps!»
  • The class is going to go to the castle tomorrow.
  • They are going to see the gold crown.
  • It might be hot in the castle.
  • The children are going to climb a lot of steps.

7

Speaking task. Расскажи о себе (запиши аудио).

Это устное задание. Подготовь ответ (30–60 секунд), запиши на телефон и отправь преподавателю до следующего занятия.

Plan for your speaking (use these prompts):
  • What are you going to do at the weekend?
  • What might the weather be like tomorrow?
  • Where might you go next month?
Example: “At the weekend I am going to visit my grandparents. Tomorrow it might be rainy, so I am going to take my umbrella. Next month we might go to the mountains — my dad wants to show me a wonderful view.”
