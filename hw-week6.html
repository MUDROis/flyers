Breakfast at the café
Every Saturday Holly and Harry have breakfast at a small café. Holly usually chooses a yoghurt with strawberries. Harry takes pancakes with honey and jam. Mum drinks tea without sugar, and dad has a big breakfast with eggs and olives. The food there is delicious, and the waiters are very friendly!

  • Mum drinks tea with a lot of sugar.
  • The food at the café is delicious.

7

Speaking task. Расскажи о себе (запиши аудио).

Это устное задание. Подготовь ответ (30–60 секунд), запиши на телефон и отправь преподавателю до следующего занятия.

Plan for your speaking (use these prompts):
  • What do you have for breakfast?
  • What is your favourite food?
  • Is there any food you don't like?
  • What does your family have for dinner?
Example: “For breakfast I have cereal with milk and a biscuit. My favourite food is pizza with cheese and olives. I don't like olives very much, but I love honey. For dinner my family has soup or rice with chicken.”
