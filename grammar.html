Hello! · Have got, месяцы, числа

SB стр. 2–3

Have got

Формула: I / you / we / they + have got · he / she / it + has got. Вопрос: Have you got…? Краткие ответы: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

ФормаПример
УтверждениеI've got a big family. She has got two cousins.
ОтрицаниеI haven't got a brother. He hasn't got a pet.
ВопросHave you got any cousins? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

Числа и месяцы

Числа до 1000: 356 — three hundred and fifty-six; 1000 — one thousand. Месяцы пишутся с заглавной буквы: January, February…. Порядковые: 1st first, 2nd second, 3rd third, 4th fourth, 5th fifth.

Unit 1 · Our home

SB стр. 4–9

Местоимения с every-, some-, no-, any-

Правило: -body / -one (люди), -thing (предметы), -where (места). Глагол после them — единственного числа: Everyone is here. Some- — в утверждениях; any- — в вопросах и отрицаниях; no- — в отрицательных по смыслу утверждениях.

ФормаПример
every- (все)Everyone is in the garden. I looked everywhere.
some- (что-то)There is something in my pocket. Someone is at the door.
any- (что-нибудь)Is there anything in the fridge? I can't find it anywhere.
no- (ничего)The house is empty — nobody is at home. I saw nothing = I didn't see anything.

Unit 2 · Going to town

SB стр. 12–17

Will (просьбы и обещания)

Формула: Will + подлежащее + глагол (без to). Отрицание: won't = will not. После will — начальная форма глагола.

ФормаПример
ПросьбаWill you fetch the key for me, please?
ОбещаниеI will (I'll) post it today. I won't forget!
Отказ / причинаI won't go to town because it's raining.

Before и After clauses

Правило: after — после того как; before — перед тем как. Внутри обеих частей — настоящее время.

СоюзПример
afterAfter lunch I had my music lesson.
beforeYou put stamps on a letter before you post it.

Unit 3 · Eating out

SB стр. 20–25

Be, look, sound, feel, taste, smell + like

Правило: like — «похож на, имеет вид/вкус/запах чего-то». What's … like? — «Какой он/она?»

ФормаПример
be likeWhat's your new house like? — It's big and light.
look likeGeorge looks like his brother.
sound likeThat sounds like your telephone ringing.
smell likeThat smells like pizza cooking.
taste likeThis tastes like banana ice cream.
feel likeThis table feels like wood, but it's plastic.

Make somebody / something + adjective

ФормаПример
make + me + adjThat smell makes me hungry!
make + sb + adjLoud music makes her tired. Games make children happy.

Made of (из чего сделано)

ФормаПример
is/are made ofThe chopsticks are made of wood. The fork is made of metal.
ВопросWhat is the scarf made of? — It's made of wool.

Unit 4 · At school

SB стр. 28–33

What time…? и время суток

Как сказать время: 7:00 — seven o'clock · 7:15 — quarter past seven · 7:30 — half past seven · 7:45 — quarter to eight · 12:00 день — midday · 12:00 ночь — midnight. a.m. — до полудня, p.m. — после полудня.

ФормаПример
ВопросWhat time does art club start? What time is morning break?
ОтветIt starts at quarter past four. Break is at half past ten.

Unit 5 · A day out

SB стр. 40–45

Be going to (планы)

Формула: am / is / are + going to + глагол. Используем для планов и намерений на будущее.

ФормаПример
УтверждениеI'm going to go to Egypt. He's going to go on an aeroplane. I'm going to ride my bike every day.
ОтрицаниеI'm not going to eat octopus. He isn't going to go to the beach.
ВопросWhat are you going to do next week? Is he going to walk to school? — No, he isn't.

As … as (сравнение)

ФормаПример
not as … asAn elephant isn't as big as a dinosaur.
as … as?Is that bracelet as expensive as the necklace?
as … asDo that as quickly as you can!

Unit 6 · Dream jobs

SB стр. 48–53

Could (мог бы)

Формула: could + глагол (без to). Не меняется по лицам: I could, she could, they could.

ФормаПример
ВозможностьI could visit the museum. They could go to the playground.
ПредложениеShe could buy soap at the chemist's.

Would like to (хотел бы)

ФормаПример
УтверждениеI would like to be a pilot. She would like to visit the museum.
ВопросWould you like to fly a plane? — Yes, I would!

Unit 7 · At the castle

SB стр. 56–61

Might (возможно)

Формула: might + глагол (без to). Выражает неуверенное предположение: «может быть».

ФормаПример
ПредположениеYou might need your coat today.
План «может быть»We might go to the beach tomorrow.

Be going to в вопросах

ФормаПример
ВопросIs he going to walk to school? — No, he isn't. What are you going to do?

Направления

Слова: north (север), south (юг), east (восток), west (запад) · go past (идти мимо), turn left/right (повернуть налево/направо), go straight on (идти прямо), across (через), through (сквозь).

Unit 8 · Sports day!

SB стр. 64–69

Present perfect: just, already, yet

Формула: have / has + V3 (третья форма глагола). just — только что · already — уже (утверждения) · yet — ещё (не) / уже (ли) — в отрицаниях и вопросах, в конце предложения.

ФормаПример
justI've just had my lunch.
alreadyI've already had my lunch. You have already done your homework.
yet (–)The children haven't had their snack yet. The competition hasn't started yet.
yet (?)Have you finished yet?
beenShe's been to the cinema this week. We haven't finished reading the book.

Unit 9 · Our camping adventure

SB стр. 76–81

Past continuous

Формула: was / were + V-ing. Длительное действие в прошлом: was — I/he/she/it, were — you/we/they.

ФормаПример
was + V-ingI was feeling happy. The sun was shining brightly. The teacher was explaining the homework.
were + V-ingThe children were walking to school.

While

Правило: while — «в то время как»: в части с while — Past continuous, во второй части часто Past simple.

ФормаПример
whileWhile Harry was walking through the woods, he hurt his foot.

Unit 10 · A good year!

SB стр. 84–89

If clauses (условные предложения)

Правило: if — «если». После if — настоящее время (даже про будущее).

ФормаПример
If + presentIf it's cold, I put on a coat. If it's sunny, they go to the beach.
If + stormIf there's a storm, she's frightened.
ВопросWhat do you do if it's foggy?

Shall we…? (предложение)

ФормаПример
ПредложениеShall we go to the cinema this afternoon?
Вопрос о планеWhat shall we have for dinner?

Unit 11 · Our summer holidays

SB стр. 92–97

Present perfect: Have you ever…?

Правило: ever — «когда-либо». В ответе: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. V3 неправильных глаголов: fly–flown, see–seen, ride–ridden, eat–eaten, be–been, swim–swum.

ФормаПример
ВопросHave you ever flown in a plane? Have you ever seen the pyramids?
ОтветYes, I have — twice! / No, I haven't, but I have ridden an elephant!

Should / shouldn't (советы)

ФормаПример
shouldYou should take your books on holiday. We should wear sunglasses today.
shouldn'tYou shouldn't put all your money in your rucksack.
ВопросShould we take our hats and gloves when we go skiing?

Unit 12 · Past and future

SB стр. 100–105

Past simple (повторение) + будущее

Правило: прошедшее — V2 / didn't + V1 (100 years ago people lived, they didn't have computers). Будущее: be going to — планы, will — предсказания и обещания.

ФормаПример
Past simplePeople lived 100 years ago. They didn't have computers.
be going toI am going to ride my bike every day. (план)
willMaybe people will live on other planets one day. I won't go to town because it's raining. (прогноз / обещание)
Present perfect + yetI haven't finished my project yet.

Tag questions (вопросы с хвостиком)

Правило: утверждение → хвостик отрицательный; отрицание → хвостик положительный.

ФормаПример
can → can't…?She can run fast, can't she?
do → don't…?They play football very well, don't they?
isn't → is…?It isn't very warm today, is it?
aren't → are…?You aren't going to be late, are you?

Where clauses

ФормаПример
whereHere's a map of the town where we live.
